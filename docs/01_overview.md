# BOND feasibility tests: overview

**BOND** (Boltzmann Occupancy Network Discovery) replaces thresholded motif matches with a
statistical-mechanics occupancy score:

    P(site bound) = sigmoid(mu - E + log a)      promoter occupancy = sum over positions and strands

| Symbol | Meaning | Source here |
|---|---|---|
| E | relative binding energy of a site (kT), 0 = consensus | JASPAR 2024 PWM log-odds (E = S_max − S), or ProBound ΔΔG/RT |
| mu | TF chemical potential (TF activity); slides all sites together | fixed grid (Tests 1–2) or learned (Tests 1, 3) |
| a | accessibility in (0, 1]; soft modulation, not a mask | ENCODE ATAC-seq peak signal |
| E_thr | FIMO-style energy cutoff for "a hit" (background p < 1e-4) | per motif, from promoter base composition |

Three small tests, each aimed at one question.

## Test 1: synthetic sanity check (`experiments/test1_synthetic.py`)
- **Question:** When data really are generated by occupancy, does the occupancy score find regulators that a thresholded scan misses? Can mu be recovered from expression alone?
- **Data:** simulated. 1,500 random promoters, 20 real JASPAR motifs, 40 conditions, 3 seeds. Each TF gets 30 targets with one strong site and 30 targets with six sub-threshold ("weak") sites. Expression is the weighted sum of exact occupancies plus noise.
- **Compared:** PWM max score; PWM hit count (p<1e-4); static occupancy (mu = E_thr−4, −2, 0); co-expression with a noisy TF proxy; motif-pruned co-expression (SCENIC-like); BOND fit (learns mu per TF per condition and w per TF).
- **Metrics:** AUROC and AUPRC× over all TF–gene pairs; separate AUROCs for strong and weak targets; recall at K = number of true pairs; Spearman correlation between learned and true mu.

## Test 2: one real cell type, K562 (`experiments/test2_k562.py`)
- **Question:** On real data, does occupancy predict (a) where a TF binds and (b) which genes respond when the TF is knocked down, better than motif scores?
- **Data:** 7,839 one-kb upstream promoters (genes measured in Perturb-seq) and 197 TFs that have both a JASPAR motif and K562 ChIP-seq. K562 ATAC. Replogle 2022 genome-wide Perturb-seq pseudobulk.
- **2a. Binding:** each score vs ChIP-peak overlap, AUROC and AUPRC× per TF. Baselines: PWM max, PWM hits, PWM hits inside ATAC peaks (the standard motif-in-peak filter), accessibility alone.
- **2b. Weak sites:** among promoters with zero thresholded hits, does occupancy from sub-threshold sites alone still predict binding?
- **2c. Held-out TFs:** 5 folds split by TF. Compares a hub prior (how often other TFs bind the gene), occupancy, and logistic regression with and without occupancy.
- **2d. Function:** knockdown response (|z| ≥ 0.5) for 21 TFs that pass filters. Adds the ChIP-bound indicator and leave-TF-out co-response correlation as baselines. Also reports binding-vs-function overlap statistics and a direct-edge proxy (bound AND responsive).

## ProBound add-on: free-energy models (`experiments/test2b_probound_vs_jaspar.py`)
- **Question:** Does replacing the approximate JASPAR energies with ProBound free-energy models (MotifCentral v1.0.0; Rube et al. 2022) improve the results?
- **What changes:** only the energy matrix. Promoters, ChIP labels, ATAC, the occupancy formula and the mu grid (relative to each model's own E_thr) stay the same. `test2_k562.py ... --model probound` and `test3_cell_state_mu.py --model probound` rerun the full pipelines.
- **Comparison:** paired per TF on the 122 K562 TFs that have both models (Wilcoxon signed-rank). The held-out-TF and function analyses rerun on the same common set (13 TFs pass the function filters). Test 3 reruns on the 90 TFs that have ProBound + ChIP in both lines.

## Test 3: cell-state mu, K562 vs HepG2 (`experiments/test3_cell_state_mu.py`)
- **Question:** Does a learned per-cell-line mu behave like TF activity?
- **Model:** y[g,c] = b_c + Σ_t w_t · occ[g,t,c](mu[t,c], accessibility in cell line c), with y = log1p(TPM) from ENCODE total RNA-seq. The model is fit on 80% of genes and evaluated on the held-out 20%.
- **Variants:** accessibility-only linear model; mu fixed; mu shared across cell lines; mu per cell line (L2 strength 1e-3, plus a weakly regularized 1e-6 sensitivity run).
- **Validation:** Δmu (HepG2 − K562) for 131 TFs is compared against independent proxies: ChIP peak-count ratio, ChIP promoter-binding ratio, and TF mRNA ratio. Significance uses a permutation test, and known marker TFs are listed.

## Prototype: GRN as the transition operator of a latent dynamical system (`experiments/latent_cascade.py`)
- **Question:** If the GRN is the coupling matrix W of a latent ODE trained to propagate state forward in time, does W leave out the spurious direct A→C edge in an A→B→C cascade? And do early velocities predict later states beyond the current state (the Markov assumption)?
- **Data:** toy only. 200 simulated cells, each a 41-point trajectory with known Hill kinetics, process noise and measurement noise. 150 cells train, 50 test, 3 seeds.
- **Model:** a per-gene encoder and decoder around latent dynamics dz/dt = W·sigmoid(a(z−c)) − γz + b, with no self-edges, trained with K-step rollouts and an L1 penalty on W. Edges are called if their strength is ≥ 10% of the strongest edge.
- **Conditions:**
  - all genes observed, at 5% and 20% measurement noise;
  - B unobserved with no spare latent unit;
  - B unobserved with one free latent unit, initialised per window from the data;
  - B unobserved with a free unit that starts from a constant and is rolled out from t = 0;
  - a Markov positive control in which B starts at a random level independent of A.
- **Ablations:** rollout horizon K (10 vs 1) crossed with an L1 grid. Baselines are pooled correlation, lagged correlation, pairwise Granger and Lasso-VAR.
- **Markov test:**
  - *Model-free:* gradient-boosting ΔR² for predicting the state 5 steps ahead when the velocity from 3 steps earlier is added to the current state, with CV grouped by cell.
  - *Model-based:* the same ΔR² computed on the trained model's forecast residuals.
- **Knockdown follow-up (Q3):** B stays unmeasured, and 50% or 25% of cells carry a B knockdown (B production × 0.1). The model either ignores the knockdown labels (`unmodeled`) or applies the knockdown to its free unit h0 with a learned efficiency (`modeled`). All of these runs roll out from t = 0 with a constant-initialised h0.
- Results: `docs/04_latent_cascade_results.md`. Run: `python experiments/latent_cascade.py run <condition> <seed>`, then `python experiments/latent_cascade.py report`.

## BOND-D: perturbation-aware dynamics + binding prior (`experiments/bond_dynamics.py`)
- **Question:** Does combining a knockdown-aware latent ODE with a sequence-derived occupancy prior beat either part alone, across edge recovery, indirect- and decoy-edge rejection, sign accuracy, and prediction of an unseen knockdown?
- **Data:** toy only. 10 genes (TFs T0–T3, targets g4–g9) and 9 true edges, including a cascade, a feed-forward loop and two repressors. Wild-type cells plus T0, T1 and T3 knockdowns are used for training; the T2 knockdown is held out. Promoters carry planted sites for each true edge (strong or weak-cluster) plus 4 decoy strong sites with no function.
- **Model variants:** base, prior, perturb, prior+perturb, shuffled-prior+perturb, and two mechanism checks that also train on T2 knockdowns (with and without the prior). Each runs in two conditions: all genes observed, and T1 never measured.
- **Baselines:** |corr|, lagged correlation, Lasso-VAR, knockdown mean difference (total effect), prior alone, and knockdown mean difference × prior.
- Results: `docs/05_bond_dynamics_results.md`. Run: `python experiments/bond_dynamics.py run <condition> <seed> <variants>`, then `baselines`, then `report`.

## Medium synthetic + real-data tests (`experiments/medium_synthetic.py`, `experiments/replogle_heldout.py`)
- **Medium synthetic:** 80 simulated genes (70 measured) with snapshot cells, Poisson counts, noisy labelling velocity, a hidden confounder, misspecified Hill shapes and a noisy sequence prior. 10 seeds. Covers ablations, baselines, prior and noise sweeps, and coverage with random, degree-based and active knockdown selection. Results: `docs/06_medium_synthetic_results.md`.
- **Real data:** Replogle K562 Perturb-seq pseudobulk, with held-out knockdown prediction and edge checks, with and without K562 ChIP or JASPAR occupancy priors. Results: `docs/07_replogle_heldout_results.md`.
